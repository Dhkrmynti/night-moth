## Direction contract

THESIS: The leaderboard is a lit sheet in a dark field; rank is distance from the lamp. Refuses the memecoin template (mascot left, headline right, stat cards, table).

OWN-WORLD: Night field #070a0e to grass #0d1611; mercury-lit cotton sheet #e9ebf3 with weave texture and soft creases, the main reading surface; lamp violet #8b5cff only for the lamp, its light and the primary action; pollen yellow #f3c74a for ETH amounts; moth-dust browns; ember red #e0523a only for flew off. Bricolage Grotesque for what is said, Fragment Mono for every number, label and leader-line callout. Moths are drawn SVG specimens; callouts are hairline leader lines with values.

STORY: Visitor sees ten moths on the sheet and understands: the ten nearest the light are paid ETH, flying off once is forever. Believes it because ranks, meter and log are live. Buys, or checks their own moth.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night. Cord across the top, lamp hanging top-centre throwing a cone. The sheet hangs from the cord and fills 70% of width on desktop (right two-thirds), full width on phone. No. 1 rests just under the lamp, 2-5 in an inner ring, 6-10 outer ring, each with a leader-line callout (rank, name, balance). Left column on desktop (top on phone): headline "Stay in the light.", one-line rule, the lamp-charge scale (escrow / next step), Buy $MOTH (violet) and Find your moth. The gecko (dev wallet) on the lamp post.
